# Exposure and analysis chronology

This is a retrospective benchmark using public historical data. It is not a preregistered or historically blinded study.

- Earlier researcher pilot and revision results had been available before the present implementation. The base modeling literature also includes CORE benchmark use.
- All raw CORE measurements were parsed for exact source-preservation and chemical identity checks. This makes a claim of no access to reserved raw measurements inappropriate.
- Eleven development profiles were used to fix the score scale, assay separability thresholds and fixed 52-target panel. The remaining 47 profiles in 44 origin groups were reserved for the present fixed analysis allocation.
- Numerical development v1 and v2 exposed failed optimization cases. The final v3 tolerances were chosen from feasibility diagnostics and production controls before computing the new CORE performance comparisons.
- All 201 v3 development optimizations passed before the first new development performance summaries were examined. The development contrasts did not establish ordinal superiority, and their positive or negative direction was not a criterion for proceeding.
- The descriptive range-width and constant-prediction reporting addition followed development outcome inspection. It changed no predictions, panel, outcome comparisons, or numerical thresholds.
- Canonical PROTOCOL_LOCK.json records the exact time and hashes before running the new reserved optimization/evaluation. This local analysis lock is not external registration, an institutional approval, or author approval for submission.
